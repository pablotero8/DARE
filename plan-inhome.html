The engagement

COMPLETE

This is not a coaching programme. Erika and Daniel are physically present at your residence or private facility daily — training you, preparing your food, and managing every variable of your health protocol in real time.

No app to check. No meals to prepare. No decisions to make. Your only task is to show up and follow through.

Designed for those for whom time is the primary constraint and results are non-negotiable.
