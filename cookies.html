Cookie Policy

Version 2026-07-10 · Last updated 10 July 2026

The short version

This site uses no advertising, analytics or tracking cookies. We do not need to show you a cookie consent banner because we only use essential technical storage, which is exempt from consent under the EU ePrivacy rules.

What we actually store in your browser

NameTypePurposeDuration
dare-langlocalStorage (technical)Remembers your language choice (English / Spanish)Until you clear your browser data
dare_token, dare_clientsessionStorage (technical)Keeps your client-portal session while the tab is openDeleted when you close the tab

None of these identify you to third parties or track your browsing on other sites.

Third-party resources

Some pages load fonts and libraries from CDNs (Google Fonts, jsDelivr, Cloudflare, unpkg). These requests expose your IP address to the CDN as any web request does, but we set no third-party cookies through them.

If this ever changes

If we ever add analytics or marketing tools, we will first show a consent banner that blocks those cookies until you accept, and update this policy.
